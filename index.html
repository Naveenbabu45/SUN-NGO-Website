About Us

A Movement Born from Purpose

Founded by three passionate individuals — Yashwanth, Krishnaprasad, and Pavan — SUN Foundation began as a mission to make a difference. When a school principal asked "Who are you?", it sparked a journey of identity, purpose, and community transformation.
